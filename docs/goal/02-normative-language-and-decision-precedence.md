## 2. Normative language and decision precedence

`MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `REQUIRED`, `SHALL`, `SHALL NOT`, `SHOULD`, `SHOULD NOT`, `MAY` and `OPTIONAL` are normative when capitalised.

Apply precedence in this order:

1. direct current operator instruction and non-overridable host safety policy;
2. this `GOAL.md`;
3. the current repository `AGENTS.md` execution shim;
4. validated current semantic source and constraints;
5. tracked architecture decisions and schemas;
6. current work packets and dependency manifests;
7. source code, tests, historical repositories and generated reports as evidence or realisation state.

A lower level cannot silently override a higher level.
